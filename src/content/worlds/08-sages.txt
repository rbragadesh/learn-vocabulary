😩📜 | After a week of searching for an apprentice, he looked [haggard].
💪🪓 | First came a [burly] blacksmith's son. "I'm strong!" he said.

🦒😅 | Then a [lanky] boy who tripped over the doorstep.
🐈🤸 | Then a [lithe] acrobat who flipped into the room.
🦵🏃 | Then a [sinewy] runner who had raced up the mountain.
🤍😰 | Last came {Name}, [pallid] with nerves, and Ziggy, who looked [wan] from flying all night.

🌧️🎻 | He played only [lugubrious] songs on his violin.

🃏🐉 | The king invited Ziggy to try to cheer him up. "A little [levity] might help," said the king.
💬😏 | Ziggy began with some friendly [banter]: "Your crown is crooked, Your Highness."

😄🤡 | Then Ziggy juggled cream pies. The cook began to [chortle].
🤣💥 | When a pie landed on the king's head, the whole court began to [guffaw]!
🃏😊 | "It was only in [jest]!" said Ziggy.
😂🎉 | And the prince? He laughed so hard he cried. From then on, the castle was full of [mirth].
